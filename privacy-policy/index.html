Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026  ·  Applies to: Sudoku Cube (Android)

Sudoku Cube does not require an account, does not collect your name or email address, and does not sell any personal data. Your game progress is stored locally on your device.

1. Who We Are

Sudoku Cube is developed and published by virt-services ("we", "us", "our"). If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at support@virt-services.com.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your puzzle progress, solved puzzles, and game statistics
  • Your settings and preferences (theme, language, text size, etc.)
  • Hint usage and daily puzzle completion records
  • Ad frequency counters (number of puzzles solved between interstitial ads)

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly enable cloud sync (a future optional feature).

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

Sudoku Cube displays ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). AdMob may collect and use data to serve personalised or non-personalised ads, depending on your consent choice.

Before showing ads, the app presents a consent dialog powered by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland. You can change your consent choice at any time in Settings → Privacy Settings.

Data that AdMob may collect includes:

  • Advertising ID (resettable in Android Settings)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • Device and app information for fraud prevention
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

For full details, see Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

To opt out of personalised advertising, you can reset your Advertising ID or opt out in your device settings: Settings → Privacy → Ads.

4. In-App Purchases — Google Play

Any purchases made within the app are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment information. Google Play's privacy policy applies to all transactions: Google Play Privacy Policy.

5. Daily Puzzles

The Daily Puzzle feature downloads one pre-generated puzzle file per day from this site, at https://sudoku-cube.virt-services.com/daily/<date>.json. The request is an ordinary anonymous HTTP GET for a static file. It includes your IP address as a standard part of any HTTP connection. No personal identifiers, no device identifier, and no game data are sent, and no request logs are retained beyond standard CDN infrastructure TTLs.

Once downloaded, the puzzle is cached on your device so it can be replayed offline. If the file cannot be reached, the app generates the day's puzzle locally instead — the Daily Puzzle keeps working with no network at all.

5a. Daily Leaderboard — not yet active

This feature is not live. No leaderboard server is currently running, no solve data leaves your device today, and the app treats the leaderboard as unavailable. This section describes what will happen if and when we turn it on, so the disclosure is in place before any data is ever sent.

When active, finishing a Daily Puzzle would submit to https://sudoku-cube.virt-services.com/api/leaderboard:

  • A random identifier generated on your device the first time you complete a daily. It is not linked to your name, email, Google account, or advertising ID, and it is not shared with any third party.
  • The puzzle date, your solve time, and your move count.

Nothing else. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app discards the identifier permanently, which also ends any ability to connect past entries to your device. We will update this page and the Google Play Data safety form before the leaderboard is enabled.

6. Notifications

The app can send you three independent kinds of notifications, each controlled by an independent toggle in Settings → Notifications. All three default to OFF on first install. The app does not ask for notification permission until you turn on at least one toggle (Android 13+ lazy permission request).

Daily Reminder (local). A single repeating local notification, fired by your device at a time you choose. The schedule is stored on your device only — no network calls, no identifiers leave the device.

Announcements and Events (remote, via Firebase Cloud Messaging). When you enable a toggle, the app subscribes the device to the corresponding FCM topic (announcements or events) on Google's infrastructure. Subscription is topic-based, not token-based:

  • A device-scoped FCM token is generated by Google for delivery routing, but the app never reads, persists, or transmits this token. We do not store it anywhere. We do not operate a server that maps tokens to users.
  • We do not know which specific devices subscribe — we send a push to a topic name and Google's FCM service fans it out to all currently subscribed devices.
  • Notification taps deep-link into named in-app routes (home, mode selection, or achievement hub). The route is encoded in the notification payload; no per-user data is included.

You can turn any toggle off at any time. Disabling Announcements or Events unsubscribes the device from the corresponding FCM topic. Disabling Daily Reminder cancels the locally scheduled notification.

Data that Firebase Cloud Messaging processes when you opt in:

  • Device-scoped FCM token (held by Google, not by us)
  • Topic membership (announcements, events)
  • IP address as a standard part of any FCM connection

For details, see Google's Firebase privacy and security information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

7. Permissions

The app requests only the permissions required to function:

  • Internet access — to load daily puzzles, display ads, and (if you opt in) receive FCM notifications
  • Network state — to check connectivity before making requests
  • Post notifications (Android 13+) — requested only when you first enable a notification toggle in Settings; not requested at app launch. Without this permission the OS will not display any notification regardless of toggle state.

No access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or files is requested.

8. Children's Privacy

Sudoku Cube is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is rated for general audiences on Google Play. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

9. Your Rights (EEA / UK Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have rights under the GDPR / UK GDPR, including:

  • The right to withdraw ad personalisation consent at any time (via Settings → Privacy Settings in the app)
  • The right to unsubscribe from FCM Announcements / Events at any time (Settings → Notifications). Doing so unsubscribes the device from the corresponding topic on Google's side.
  • The right to disable the local Daily Reminder at any time (Settings → Notifications)
  • The right to request deletion of any data we hold — since we hold none, there is nothing to delete
  • The right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

10. Data Retention

All game data is stored on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. We do not maintain servers that store personal data. Topic-based FCM subscriptions are managed by Google's infrastructure; when you uninstall the app or disable a notification toggle, the device is unsubscribed and Google ceases delivery to that device.
