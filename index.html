Frequently Asked Questions

How do shared edges work?

Cells that sit on an edge between two faces appear on both faces at once. When you place a digit in that cell, it counts toward the rows, columns, and boxes on every face it touches. This is the core mechanic that makes Sudoku Cube different from flat Sudoku.

My progress disappeared — what happened?

Progress is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data will erase it. We recommend completing a pack before clearing storage.

How do I change the language?

Go to Settings → Language and pick from the available options. The app supports English, Français, Español, Português, and more.

How do I opt out of personalised ads?

Go to Settings → Privacy Settings inside the app to change your ad consent at any time. You can also reset your Advertising ID in your Android device settings under Privacy → Ads.
